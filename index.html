02 · UX Founding Work

How I introduced UX to an engineering-led startup

This grew in parallel with the shipped features — I built the practice while designing the work, not before it.

Initiative 01 · UX practice · Valispace 2021–2022

Show, don't tell

Founded the UX Guild for designers and developers, and introduced user interviews and prototype testing by doing them.

Initiative 02 · Consistency · Valispace 2021–2022

One look and feel

Design principles and one look and feel across product, API docs and marketing, in collaboration with the marketing team.

Initiative 03 · Leadership · Valispace 2021–2022

From UX to Product

The approach became company practice and I was promoted onto the new Product team. I later drafted the role and hired my successor, still at Altium 3.5 years later.

Initiative 04 · Accessibility · Valispace 2021–2022

Accessibility as essential

Flagged a low-contrast red–green diagram colour-blind users later reported. Argued WCAG-aware design as essential in technical software, not nice-to-have.

04 · Background

Background

Experience

UX / UI Designer, Senior and leadership · Valispace (acquired by Altium) 2021–23

Aerospace systems and requirements engineering software · Lisbon, Portugal and Bremen, Germany (hybrid)

  • Secured a six-figure development contract with user stories and hi-fi Figma prototypes. Those features ship in Altium 365 today.
  • First designer in the company. Built the design practice from scratch while learning the domain, and stayed sole designer through the 20 to 50 growth phase before joining the new Product team.
  • Founded a cross-functional UX guild. Drafted the role and led the hire of the senior designer who took the work forward, still at Altium 3.5 years later.
  • Brought user-centred methods into an engineering-led team: user stories, journeys, wireframes and interactive prototypes, for highly regulated aerospace software.
Product Designer · Independent Practice 2025–now

Berlin, Germany (remote)

  • Nominated for the Mozilla Fellowship 2026, passed round 1, for Navigator.
  • Navigator: concept and prototype for an EU AI Act and GDPR compliance tool that makes legal requirements usable for musicians.
  • Also shaped the learnability and design of System Forge, a SysML v2 graph-modelling tool for systems engineers, and improved the MDN Web Audio API docs.
Accessibility Facilitator · ALB gGmbH 2024–25

Berlin, Germany (onsite)

  • Supported autistic adults with technology and daily life, and saw first-hand where accessibility compliance and lived reality diverge.
  • Designed a photography programme using UX methods. It led to a workshop invitation from Alice Salomon Hochschule Berlin.
UX Researcher · Eigensonne 2023

Berlin, Germany (hybrid)

  • UX research for a solar-sales website. Role ended when the company closed.
Specialized Programs Support, Creative Media Team · Media Products and Pro Apps · Apple 2016–20

Apple, European Headquarters, Cork, Ireland (onsite)
