Berliner Luft

When and where are people in Berlin most exposed to air pollution?

Context
Independent project, 2026. Open city data.
Scope
Data exploration, sketch, interface, prototype.
Data
Hourly values, 15 stations, 2025. NO₂ and PM2.5.
Published
20 October 2026 on Hugging Face (opens in a new tab).

Impact

  • 0 of 15stations above the EU limit in force
  • 5 of 15above the EU 2030 limit
  • 10 of 15above the WHO guideline

NO₂ annual mean, 2025. Public sources usually show one limit and do not name it. The interface shows all three.

Interface

Two inputs: time and stations. Both charts update from them.

  • NO₂gas, mainly traffic
  • PM2.5fine particles
  • ■ ● ◆Square, circle, diamondtraffic, background, suburb
  • Dotted, dashed, solid lineWHO, EU 2030, EU in force

After

  2. Choose stations
  3. Read all stations against three limits

Data exploration

NO₂ peaks in the morning rush hour. PM2.5 changes little during the day. A daily curve shows both, so the interface has no hour selector.

Sketches

First build

  • StationsNames were cut off in the sidebar. They are now labeled on the map.
  • MapSymbols had no names. Map, list and chart now share one selection.
  • TimeAn hour range from 00:00 to 23:00. Now year or month, weekday or weekend.
  • LimitsOne limit at a time. Now all three in the chart.

Key decisions

  • Single screenTime, place and limits without navigation.
  • Shared selectionMap, list and chart select the same stations.
  • Labeled limitsDotted WHO, dashed EU 2030, solid EU in force.
  • Gaps labeledNo PM2.5 sensor: “not measured here”.

15 stations cover 892 km². The interface shows values per station, not per district.

Next version

One area, three sources. Choose a district and see what was measured, what was modeled and what a satellite saw, each with its resolution, date and certainty.

  • Compare before choosingResolution, date and coverage side by side, as when choosing imagery before an order.
  • Gaps over timeMonths with too few valid satellite days are greyed, with the reason.
  • Area summaryStations inside or the nearest one, grid mean and highest cell, covering pixels.
  • No value without a sourceEmpty stays empty. Satellite data is regional context, not street air.

States to design: no station in the area, area smaller than one pixel, too few valid days, model year differs from measurement year. Planned, not built.

Lessons

  1. Exploration first

    The charts showed which filters were useful.

  2. Charts as a draft

    Colors, line styles and sort order came from the exploration charts.

  3. Next

    Usability test with residents, then the next version.
