The shipped app

How it works

One use case in. The obligations and steps that apply come out.

The rules are dense and written for lawyers. The decision tree asks about AI involvement, personal data and distribution, matches the answers to the rules and returns tailored requirements and action steps.

Process

Research, structure, verify, build, publish.

  1. Researchprimary regulations, not summaries
  2. Structurerules mapped to real use cases
  3. Verifysources checked before anything ships
  4. Build & publishprototype with design tools and code · GitHub Pages

Key design decisions

How do you turn dense legislation into something a musician can act on without pretending to give legal advice? The decisions kept the tool useful and honest at once.

  • Decision tree over free texta few questions replace pages of legal reading
  • It doesn't decide for youthe tool lays out what applies and leaves the call in your hands
  • Accessibility by defaultplain language and readable structure
  • Always linked to the sourceevery result points back to the AI Act, GDPR or EAA

Opportunities · use an LLM to translate legal language into plainer terms, always linking back to the original source
