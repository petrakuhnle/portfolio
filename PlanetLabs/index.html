02 · UX Founding Work

How I introduced UX to an engineering-led startup

Valispace, 2021–2022. This grew in parallel with the shipped features — I built the practice while designing the work.

Show, don't tell

Founded the UX Guild for designers and developers, and introduced user interviews and prototype testing by working with people who were open to it and by showing genuine interest in contributions while filtering out generalized insights.

One look and feel

Design principles and one look and feel across product, API docs and marketing, in collaboration with marketing, solution management and development. Working in a fast-paced and ambiguous startup environment as first designer, I evaluated priority and for example gave input to the website look, provided marketing ressources to match colour combinations, improved the API design consistency, provided feedback on visuals in the product documentation. The main focused however remained on design of complex system-in-systems features and growing the design culture & getting a second person hired into the startup.

From UX to Product

The focus on the user experience and product increased to attention for quality of the experience and collaboration with engineering, product and design. I was promoted onto the new Product team. I defined the senior role for the second hire and hired my successor — who still contributes to the design culture after acquisition in Altium today.

Accessibility as a requirement

I made the case for improving the colour palette for a pie chart. We later were contacted by engineers about the insufficient colour contrast I had flagged earlier. Ensuring compliance with accessbility guidelines prevents expert users, from contacting about unusable UI elements, because of colour contrast, font size or other inflexible elements that make UI elements hard or impossible to use for individual users.

Learning the domain

I came to aerospace from four years at Apple supporting professional audio and video users, where I diagnosed problems in live production and wrote up reproducible cases for engineering, deciding case by case what was a bug and what was a UX problem. That method is how I learned systems engineering: isolate the variable, reproduce the situation with the person who has it, and separate what is broken from what is badly designed.
