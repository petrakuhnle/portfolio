05Key design decisions

How do you turn dense legislation into something a musician can act on without pretending to give legal advice? The decisions kept the tool useful and honest at once.

Decision tree over free texta few questions replace pages of legal reading
It doesn't decide for youthe tool lays out what applies and leaves the call in your hands
Accessibility by defaultplain language and readable structure
Always linked to the sourceevery result points back to the AI Act, GDPR or EAA
