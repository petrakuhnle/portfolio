Use case 2 of 4 · Valispace, 2021

Master-Follower

Keeping reused requirements in sync across projects.

Role
Only designer
Product
Valispace, 2021
Status
Shipped, live in Altium 365
← Portfolio Altium 365 documentation (opens in a new tab)

Problem

Aerospace teams reuse requirements across projects, like several aircraft models. When the original changed, engineers updated every copy by hand. Changes got lost.

What I designed

The Master is the original requirement. A Follower is its linked copy in another project. When the Master changes, the Follower is flagged. Its owner applies or rejects the change, and the decision is logged.

  • Up to date
  • Master changed
  • Follower differs
  • Not linked

My role

I translated between hardware experts and software engineers. Each week I iterated designs with experts, drafted the logic and worked with engineers until release.

Key decisions

Too simple and it is useless to engineers. Too complex and it is unclear to use.

  • Flags, not silent updates.
  • One status per Follower, in the users' words.
  • Visibility by role, view and task.

Impact

  • Six-figure contractsigned based on the designs
  • Shipped 2021, live 2026kept after the Altium acquisition
  • Rework preventedI caught a mismatch between the CPO and backend
